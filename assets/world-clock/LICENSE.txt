land-mask.bin is a deterministic 1024 x 512, one-bit-per-pixel rasterization
of that land geometry in equirectangular coordinates, from 180 W to 180 E
and 90 N to 90 S. Bits are stored least-significant-bit first.
No attribution or permission is required by Natural Earth; this notice
records the source of the geographic data.

cities.json contains representative timezone locations derived from IANA
Time Zone Database zone.tab, which is in the public domain.
Source: https://data.iana.org/time-zones/tzdb/zone.tab
The Beijing and New Delhi labels use the coordinates of those named cities.
